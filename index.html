Arnold, The Orc
Sculpted in zbrush and textured in Substance Painter. This particular character was an excellent study for anatomy a beautiful concept by Wei Wong. The armour was created in Maya and is an original concept and textured inside Substance as well.
↗
↗
Mermaid Bust
Sculpted entirely in zbrush. This piece came to be when I was introduced to Forest Roger’s works. Inspired by his fantastical creature it made me want to explore forms and how much can be communicated just with humble clay.
↗
↗
Something Fishy
Created in Maya and hand painted inside Substance Painter. This piece for me was entirely about the texture. Which is why I spent days hand painting them to deliver that nostalgia and comfort of a Ghibli movie. I wanted to create an environment which would make the viewer want to step inside. Original concept by art Mike Deng.
↗
↗
Japanese War Cart
This asset was made entirely in Maya and textured inside Substance Painter and finally rendered in maya Arnold. This asset was inspired by ancient Japanese style carts user during war time primarily to delivery food and weaponry. Original concept by Tokkun studios.

02 — ABOUT

Hi, I’m Areena Fatima — a character artist, digital sculptor, and hard-surface 3D artisan working as a freelance artist.

My work moves between organic sculpting and hard-surface modelling, realism and stylisation. I’m inspired by the world around me and the ideas that live in my imagination, and I strive to bring them into 3D — turning every concept into something tangible, expressive, and alive.
